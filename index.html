The West Haven Story

Authentic Egyptian Soul & Boutique Serenity

Nestled amidst swaying date palms and emerald agricultural fields on Luxor’s storied West Bank, The West Haven Villa Luxor offers an intimate alternative to large commercial hotels.

Designed with natural sandstone tones, hand-loomed Egyptian linens, and panoramic rooftop terraces overlooking the Nile and the Theban Mountains, every detail is crafted to slow down time after your explorations of Karnak, Hatshepsut’s Temple, and the Valley of the Kings.
